# elevn promo video: storyboard draft (2026-11-19)

Status: proposal only. Nothing generated, no credits spent. Files for this work live in this folder.

Chosen by default because the owner had no preference on which video to make. The Katana promo
is postponed until the platform is ready (HANDOFF), so this is a plan to approve, not a render.

## The video

- 15 seconds, 16:9 (a 9:16 cut can follow), for X (https://x.com/elevndotfun).
- Look: night match in glass and steel. Near-black, warm white, trophy gold as the only accent.
  The glass ball with the gold core is the hero. No blue light. No Tokyo look, no rain, no neon.
- Words: one-word title cards in Barlow Condensed, drawn in front of the ball and clear of it.
- Honest claims only: "Play free", Blitz matchday, Draft weekly, top 5 paid. No promises of winnings.

## Ways to make it, cheapest first

1. **Code capture (no credits).** Record the home-page prototype's beats in the container's
   Chromium and cut them in ffmpeg. Software-rendered WebGL is slow, so capture frame by frame
   (the `?still=1&p=` option) and stitch. Cost: 0.
2. **Higgsedit title cards (code-based, sandbox).** Title cards and the closing frame over the
   captured ball. Historic price 0 credits, to be re-quoted.
3. **Katana remix (generative, paid).** Last. Historic rate 7 credits per second at 720p, so
   about 210 to 265 credits for 15 s before retries. Needs a live quote and a hard cap from the owner.
