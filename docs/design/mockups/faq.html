Help

Short answers about playing, prizes and getting paid.

FAQ

Getting started

Can I try it without a wallet?

Yes. Everything works as a guest, including building squads and trying Draft. Nothing is saved. Connect a wallet to save your squads and pick a username.

What do I need to play for prizes?

Holding 150,000 $ELEVN unlocks Matchday Blitz. Holding 300,000 in all also unlocks Draft. It is one entry per wallet, however much it holds, and you can play as soon as the wallet holds the tokens.

Does it cost anything to enter?

No. There is no entry payment. You play free; you hold the token.

Matchday Blitz

How do squads work?

Each matchday you build 3 squads on any 3 fixtures you choose. A squad is 3 players from one fixture, with at least 1 from each club. Your captain scores double points. Each squad locks at its own kick-off.

When is a result final?

15 minutes after the last match of the day.

Draft

How do the packs work?

Draft is the luck mode; Blitz is the skill mode. Your packs are personal and random: 5 packs of 3 players, different for every wallet.

What makes a valid team, and when does it play?

A five-a-side with at least 1 defender, 1 midfielder and 1 attacker, no goalkeepers, and at most 2 players from any one club. Your captain scores double points. Draft opens on Tuesday, locks at Friday's first kick-off, and scores Friday to Monday.

What if a match is postponed?

If a player's match is postponed or cancelled, the player is swapped for your highest-valued unused player in the same position. If you do not make the swap yourself, it happens automatically. If no unused player in that position is left, the slot scores 0.

Prizes

Who gets paid?

The top 5 on every board: Blitz matchday, Blitz weekly ($500) and Draft weekly. The split is 50 / 25 / 12 / 8 / 5 percent.

How is the weekly $500 won?

Your best 3 matchday totals in the week are added up. The Blitz week runs Tuesday to Monday, UK time, and the top 5 are paid.

What happens in a tie?

More captain points wins. If that is level too, the prize is split.

What if fewer than 3 people enter?

The pot rolls forward to the next one.

How do I get my prize?

Press Collect on Winnings. Half is yours at once. The other half goes into Locked, and you can withdraw 20% of it once every 24 hours. The timer starts at your first collect and restarts at each withdrawal. Days you miss do not add up.

What does Frozen mean?

Your wallet holds less than 150,000 $ELEVN. Locked and its timer pause until the wallet holds that much again. Nothing is forfeited.

Which currency are prizes paid in?

SOL on devnet during the test season.

Other

What is Penalty Shootout?

A side game with no prize and no holding requirement. You get 10 ranked games a day and unlimited practice.

Who can post in chat?

Any connected wallet that holds the tokens.
