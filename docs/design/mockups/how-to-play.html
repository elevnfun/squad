New here? Start here

Eight short steps from your first look to your first prize. You can try everything before you connect.

How to play

  1. 1

    Try it as a guest

    Everything works without a wallet. Build squads, look at sample packs and open the boards. Nothing is saved until you connect.

    Guest · nothing saved
  2. 2

    Connect a wallet and pick a username

    One entry per wallet, however much it holds. Your username is what other players see.

    sample_user
  3. 3

    Hold $ELEVN to play

    Hold 150,000 $ELEVN to play Matchday Blitz. Hold 300,000 to play Draft too. You can play as soon as your wallet holds the tokens.

    Blitz150,000
    Blitz + Draft300,000
  4. 4

    Blitz: three squads a matchday

    • Pick any 3 fixtures on the matchday.
    • Each squad is 3 players from one fixture, with at least 1 from each club.
    • Choose a captain. The captain scores double.
    • Each squad locks at its own kick-off.
    • One live leaderboard for the matchday.
    Player×2CaptainPlayerLocks at kick-off
  5. 5

    Draft: five packs, one five-a-side

    • You get 5 personal packs of 3 players, different for every wallet. Luck decides who.
    • Build a five-a-side: at least 1 DEF, 1 MID and 1 ATT, no goalkeepers, at most 2 players per club.
    • Choose a captain. The captain scores double.
    • Opens Tuesday, locks at Friday's first kick-off, scores Friday to Monday.
    DEFMIDATTat least 1 of each
  6. 6

    Prizes: every board pays the top 5

    Blitz daily, the fixed $500 Blitz weekly prize 3.13 SOL and Draft weekly. Prizes are in SOL. Results are final 15 minutes after the day's last match.

    1st 50%2nd 25%3rd 12%4th 8%5th 5%
  7. 7

    Collecting your winnings

    Press Collect and half is yours at once. The other half is Locked: you can withdraw 20% of it once every 24 hours. If your wallet holds less than 150,000 $ELEVN, Locked and its timer are frozen until it holds enough again. Nothing is forfeited.

    Collect now0.50 SOL$80
    Locked0.50 SOL$8020% every 24 hours

    Frozenif you hold under 150,000

  8. 8

    Penalty Shootout, just for fun

    A side game with no prize and no holding needed. Ten ranked games a day, and unlimited practice.

    Try Penalty Shootout

Questions about prizes or holding? The FAQ in the menu has the details.
