What is elevn

Fantasy football with a result at full time.

About

elevn is fantasy football where the result lands at full time, not at the end of a long season. There are two modes, Matchday Blitz and Draft, with daily and weekly prizes paid to the top 5 on every board. Access is token-gated on Solana: you hold $ELEVN to play, and you pay no entry fee. The test season runs on devnet, so prizes are in test SOL.

Two modes

Skill mode

Blitz

Matchday Blitz is the main mode. Pick 3 squads on any 3 fixtures of the day and watch them score live. Each squad locks at its own kick-off, and the daily prize is paid after the last match.

  • 3 squads of 3 players
  • Captain scores double
  • Daily board and a $500 weekly prize
Luck mode

Draft

Weekly Draft deals you 5 personal, random packs of 3 players. Choose a five-a-side from them and let it score from Friday to Monday.

  • 5 packs, a five-a-side team
  • Captain scores double
  • One weekly board, top 5 paid

Launch leagues

  • Premier League
  • La Liga
  • Bundesliga
